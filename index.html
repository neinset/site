The Grief Complex About

This is nothing but a mode of communication. Low cost, low value, low fidelity - rare as in "way less popular than you". It's a one-way affair where I pretend to be unclear about a lot of things while trusting you get the picture.

Neinset is the name of a faceless collective spearheaded, largely, by ottiliere, and coordinated, maybe, by someone else. Guest stars run the gamut; our neighbors, your distant relatives, old timer big name fans of webcomics you'd probably rather forget. But we remember. Oh, how we remember.

What we do here is secret - that is to say, no one knows unless you tell them. That isn't to say we don't want you to tell anyone, but...you know.
